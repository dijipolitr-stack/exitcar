Why a Car in Kemer?

🗺️

Ancient Sites Are Spread Out

Olympos, Phaselis, Yanartaş and Adrasan are spread out; reaching them by bus or dolmuş eats your day.

🏖️

Pine-Forest Beaches

Beldibi, Göynük, Kemer beach, Çamyuva — each has its own character. With a car you can sample 3-4 beaches a day.

⛰️

Close to the Taurus Mountains

Tahtalı Peak (Olympos cable car), Göynük Canyon, the Kuzdere villages — a mountain escape just 30-40 minutes from the beach.

3 Places You Must See in Kemer

🔥 Yanartaş (Chimaera)

A natural gas seeping from the mountainside has been burning for thousands of years — visit at night for the full effect.

🏛️ Ancient Phaselis

A Lycian city with three harbours, aqueducts, an ancient theatre and seaside walking paths. 15 km from Kemer.

🏔️ Tahtalı Mountain Cable Car

Mediterranean views from 2,365 m. The cable car ride is about 10 minutes; the peak has a restaurant and viewing deck.

Kemer Car Rental — FAQ

Can you deliver the car to my hotel in Kemer?

Yes. Free delivery is available to most large hotels in central Kemer and Çamyuva. A small delivery fee may apply for further points like Tekirova or Çıralı. Add your hotel name at booking.

Is the coastal road (D400) safe with children?

Generally safe but winding. Add a child seat, travel by day, don't cut to the mountain side. Don't rush in summer holiday traffic — and enjoy the views.

Which car class is best for Kemer?

A compact or mid-size sedan suits the coast. A small SUV is more comfortable if you add the Olympos-Adrasan side roads. Manual handles the Tahtalı climb well; if you go automatic, pick a petrol engine.
