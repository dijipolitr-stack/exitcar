Car Rental Services Guide: All Types and Pricing Explained (2026)

Car rental is no longer just "a car for a day". Hourly use, weekly holidays, monthly business trips, long-term operational leases, transfer services — every scenario has its own cost structure and insurance requirement. This guide explains, in plain language, which service fits which scenario and what really sits behind the price tag.

1. Types of Car Rental

Hourly Rental

For short 2-12 hour needs. Inner-city meetings, shopping, a short airport run. The hourly rate is 1/4 to 1/3 of the daily rate; from half a day upwards the daily tariff usually wins.

Daily Rental (1-7 days)

Holidays, short business trips, day trips from your hotel. The most common model in Türkiye. The price is fixed the moment you place a deposit.

Weekly & Monthly Rental

8-30 day stretches. The per-day rate drops 15-20% on weekly packages and 30-40% on monthly. Ideal for summer holidaymakers and project-based workers.

Long-Term Rental (3-24 months)

Also called "operational lease". Insurance, maintenance and replacement car included. Increasingly chosen by individuals — especially new residents — as an alternative to ownership. See our Fleet Management Guide for details.

Transfer Services

A single one-way ride with a driver — airport to hotel or vice versa. Different from rental: priced by distance, not duration. AYT to Kemer transfers run roughly 1,500-2,500 TL.

2. How Pricing Works

A rental price is made of these components:

  • Base rate (car + days): Car class times duration.
  • Traffic insurance: Mandatory, included.
  • Comprehensive package: Optional — strongly recommended.
  • Add-ons: GPS, child seat, additional driver, etc.
  • Airport/one-way fee: When applicable.
  • VAT (20%): Applied to all items.
💡 Important distinction: On comparison platforms like ExitCar the price you see already includes everything. No surprises. On individual supplier sites, CDW or VAT may show on a separate line — the total is the same but you won't know it in advance.

3. Insurance Packages Explained

In Türkiye three insurance tiers are standard:

  • Basic (Free): Only traffic + mandatory liability. Insufficient alone — even a small scratch comes out of your pocket.
  • Safe / CDW (~350 TL/day): Comprehensive cover with ~2,500 TL excess. Most popular choice, sufficient for SME use.
  • All-Inclusive (~680 TL/day): Zero excess, minor damage and glass/tyres included. Ideal for foreign tourists and long routes.

4. Common Add-On Services

  • GPS / Navigation (~150 TL/day): Unnecessary if you have a phone; useful for long planned routes.
  • Baby/child seat (~120 TL/day): 9-18 kg and 9-36 kg options. Mandatory in Türkiye.
  • Additional driver (~200 TL/day): Essential on long trips — add to the contract for insurance to stay valid.
  • Mobile Wi-Fi (~180 TL/day): Useful for foreign tourists.
  • Winter tyre package (~90 TL/day): Mandatory if you're heading to the Taurus Mountains November-March.
  • 24/7 roadside assistance (~100 TL/day): Usually included in upper packages; rarely needed as a separate line.

5. Which Type Suits Whom?

  • Weekend tourist: Daily + Safe package + child seat (if applicable).
  • Foreign holidaymaker (1-2 weeks): Weekly + All-Inclusive + additional driver + GPS.
  • Monthly digital nomad: Monthly package + Safe + additional driver. 30%+ long-term discount.
  • Foreign resident in Antalya: A 6-12 month long-term lease usually makes more sense.
  • Single airport trip: Use a transfer service instead of a rental.

6. Pre-Contract Checklist

  • Take 360° photos before driving off; have every scratch, rim mark, bumper scuff noted on the form.
  • Photograph the fuel level.
  • Test the A/C and lights.
  • Confirm the HGS (motorway) account is active.
  • Clarify the return time and location (late = extra day charge).
  • Learn the CDW excess and how to report damage.
